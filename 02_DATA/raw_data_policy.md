# Raw data policy

This repository distributes derived/aggregated research outputs rather than a public dump of raw article text. News URLs and source metadata may be retained locally by the researcher for auditability, subject to source terms and applicable law.

**No social-media data are used in the current research release.**

BPKH/user-specific raw inputs must be supplied locally and must not be committed if they contain restricted, private, credentialed, or otherwise non-shareable information.
