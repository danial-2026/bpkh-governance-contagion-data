# Limitations

- The corpus is public news media and may not represent all public discourse.
- The study uses an observation window ending 3 September 2026.
- Semantic thresholds, network weights, and EWS weights are study-specific analytical design parameters rather than externally validated regulatory thresholds.
- Lead-lag correlations indicate temporal association, not causality.
- NCI measures structural connectedness, not demonstrated risk transmission.
- Isolation Forest is unsupervised; no manually labelled future-crisis ground truth is used.
- The social-media pipeline is excluded from this release.
- The EWS is decision support, not an official BPKH risk rating or a misconduct detector.
