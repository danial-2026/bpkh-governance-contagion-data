# Governance Contagion Response Protocol

**Detect → Assess → Alert → Respond → Learn**

| Signal | Suggested governance response |
|---|---|
| Green | Routine monitoring and periodic review |
| Yellow | Enhanced monitoring, source verification, mandate clarification, communication preparation |
| Red | Management review, cross-functional coordination, formal verification, mitigation and communication |

The protocol is a proposed decision-support mechanism. It does not replace BPKH enterprise-risk policies, legal review, crisis-communication protocols, or official risk appetite/limits.
