# EWS interpretation guide

## Green
Routine governance-risk monitoring. No elevated composite signal relative to the observed research baseline.

## Yellow
Enhanced monitoring condition. Recommended actions include verification of source narratives, cross-functional review, and preparation of clarification if institutional exposure is material.

## Red
Elevated analytical governance-risk environment. Recommended actions include management review, cross-functional coordination, verification of external allegations/claims, clear mandate differentiation, and timely mitigation/communication.

### Important
Green/Yellow/Red are analytical decision-support classifications based on study-specific percentile thresholds. They are not official BPKH risk ratings and are not forecasts of corruption, financial loss, or institutional failure.
