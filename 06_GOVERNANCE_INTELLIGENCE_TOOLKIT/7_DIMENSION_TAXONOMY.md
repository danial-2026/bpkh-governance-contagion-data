# Seven-dimension governance-risk taxonomy

1. **Quota_Governance** — quota allocation and quota-governance controversies.
2. **Corruption_Investigation** — alleged corruption, investigation, examination, and related enforcement narratives.
3. **Governance_Oversight** — audit, oversight, parliamentary, and institutional accountability narratives.
4. **BPKH_Governance** — BPKH governance, accountability, transparency, oversight, leadership, and institutional policy.
5. **Hajj_Fund_Management** — Hajj fund management, investment, value benefits, sukuk, liquidity, and financial-management narratives.
6. **Public_Trust_Reputation** — public confidence, reputation, criticism, concern, accountability, and transparency narratives.
7. **Clarification_Response** — institutional clarification, response, rebuttal, and governance-improvement actions.

External Crisis = dimensions 1–3. BPKH Exposure = dimensions 4–5. Trust Risk = dimension 6. Clarification/Response is treated as mitigation/response and excluded from the core contagion calculation and risk network.
